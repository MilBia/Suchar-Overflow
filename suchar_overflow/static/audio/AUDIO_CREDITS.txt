Easter-egg sound effects
========================

rimshot.wav  — a short "ba dum tss" sting
dust.wav     — a soft puff of settling dust

Both files are original works, synthesised from scratch by the Suchar Overflow
project with `scripts/generate_easter_egg_audio.py` (Python standard library
only — no external encoder, no third-party samples). The output is plain 16-bit
mono WAV and byte-deterministic, so re-running the script with unchanged
parameters regenerates them identically.

all copyright and related or neighbouring rights to these two audio files. They
are released under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain
Dedication: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

No attribution is required. There are no third-party audio assets in this
directory, so — unlike the vendored JavaScript libraries — nothing here needs to
be tracked against an upstream release.
